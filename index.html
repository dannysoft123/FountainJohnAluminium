Built around quality.

Fountain John Quality Aluminium Products Solution Limited is one of the leading aluminium solutions companies founded and based in Lagos, Nigeria.

We specialize in premium aluminium fabrication and extrusion, supply and installation to meet the unique needs of clients across the construction industry — from residential to industrial, institutional and commercial projects.

Innovation and quality sit at the heart of everything we do. Every frame, panel and finish is crafted with precision and attention to detail, resulting in timeless pieces that exceed expectations.
